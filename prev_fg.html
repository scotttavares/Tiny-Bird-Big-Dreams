Stick anything anywhere.

Dozens of die-cut stickers and strips of washi tape, ready to pile onto any page. Build a treasure map. Give a garden witch a frog for a face. It’s your book.

Drawing

Pen, pencil, marker and chalk, on paper in your choice of color and pattern. Draw on the page, or right across your photos. Two fingers to undo, three to redo.
