Common questions

How do I add someone?

On the home screen tap the + to add a person by hand, or tap Import Contacts to pick people from your address book. You can add a phone number so you can text or call them later.

Why is someone drifting outward?

Each ring is a length of time since you last connected — about two weeks, a month, three months, six months, and a year. As time passes without contact, people move to the outer rings. Reaching out (or logging an interaction) brings them back to the center.

Send a Text or Quick Call isn’t doing anything.

These open your own apps, so the contact needs a phone number saved (tap ⋯ on their profile to add one). Texting opens whichever messaging apps you have installed. Calling uses the Phone app on iPhone; on iPad, which can’t place cellular calls, it opens FaceTime instead.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap + in the corner, search for Orbit, and choose Small, Medium, or Large. The larger sizes draw your orbit; the small one shows who’s drifting at a glance.

Is my data private?

Yes. Orbit has no account and no server — everything stays on your device. See the Orbit Privacy Policy for details.

Will I lose my people if I restart my phone?

No. Your orbit is saved on your device and restored when you reopen the app. (Deleting the app removes its data, since nothing is stored anywhere else.)
