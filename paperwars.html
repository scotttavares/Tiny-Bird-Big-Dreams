Paper Wars

A pencil war on notebook paper.

Two players. Two stick-figure armies hidden on lined paper. Take turns firing in secret, spot the enemy with ink bleed, and scribble them out before they get you. Every soldier has a name, a big mouth and a very nervous stomach.

Play it now!

Free in your browser. Play a friend on one screen, or take on the computer. Best with the sound on.

Sound on. Tap the orders to begin. Open full screen

How a war goes

Hide your army

Tap to place five soldiers and a fort on your half of the page. Rocks, mountains and water change every map.

Fire in secret

Place a dot where you think they are, then fire. A hit scribbles a soldier out in red pen. A near miss can scare one so badly he toots.

Ink bleed

Every shot bleeds a little, so a miss can still spot enemies nearby. Hide behind a boulder and the ink can't reach you.

Machine gun

A couple of times a game, pick a soldier and spray a dashed line of bullets clear to the edge of the paper.

Reinforcements

Miss too often and the other side gets a fresh soldier, flown in by paper plane and parachuted onto the page.

Medals and replays

The match ends with a medal ceremony, gold trophies for the silliest moments, and a highlight video with a kill cam to share.

Coming to iPhone Duo

Paper Wars is being built for iPhone Duo. Stand the phone up like a tent between two players and each one sees only their own half of the page: no peeking, no hand-offs. The web version is the prototype we play-test with, so it may change as we go.
