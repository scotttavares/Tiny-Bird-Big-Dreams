Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Tiny Bird, Big Dreams ("we", "us", "our") is a small independent app studio. We collect as little data as we can, and nothing for analytics unless you say yes. This policy covers tinybirdbigdreams.com. Each app also has its own privacy page (for example Orbit, Paper Wars and Field Guide).

Cookies & analytics

When you first visit, a cookie banner asks whether we may measure your visit. You can change your answer any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

Before you answer, or if you say no

  • We measure nothing about your visit.
  • We store your choice in a small essential cookie (tb_consent) so we don't ask again.
  • We count banner answers (accepted, declined or custom) as an anonymous daily total, with no identifier attached.
  • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a "no".

If you say yes to analytics

  • We create a random visitor ID and keep it in your browser's local storage (tb_vid). It lets us count how many different people visit each week. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • We record page views, the website you came from (its domain only), your device type (phone, tablet or desktop), how far you scroll, and clicks on our App Store, TestFlight and "Play" links and on the interactive parts of the home page.
  • We store these as daily totals (for example "Orbit page: 40 views on Tuesday"), not as a history of what you did. The weekly visitor count keeps visitor IDs for up to 120 days; daily totals are kept for about 13 months.
  • We also load Cloudflare Web Analytics, which measures page loads without cookies.

Messages and signups

If you use the contact form, we receive and store your name, email address and message so we can reply, for up to two years. A first reply may be drafted automatically (see below) and sent with Resend. If you join "Get news when something hatches", we store your email address to tell you about new apps and games; email us to be removed. We keep these whether or not you accepted analytics cookies, because you sent them to us on purpose.

AI disclosure

We use AI tools to help us design, write and build this website and our apps, and to draft first replies to contact-form messages. People on our team guide and review the work before anything ships, and read every message. Some photos and video on this site are AI-generated illustrations of everyday moments.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare: hosts this website and stores our analytics totals, messages and signups; Cloudflare Web Analytics (only after consent).
  • Resend: sends our email replies.
  • An AI provider: classifies and drafts first replies to contact-form messages.
  • Google Fonts: serves typography.

Your choices

You can withdraw analytics consent at any time with "Cookie settings", clear local storage in your browser, or email hello@tinybirdbigdreams.com to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted.
