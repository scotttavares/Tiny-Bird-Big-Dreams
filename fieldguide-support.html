Common questions

Do I need an iPhone Duo?

Field Guide is made for iPhone Duo, where you get two pages when it’s open. On any other iPhone or iPad, choose More > Layout to see the two-page views.

How do I add stickers, photos, videos or text?

Tap the smiley face in the tool bar. Pick a photo, video, sticker or tape, or tap Add Text Box to write something.

How do I move or resize something?

Drag to move it, pinch to resize it and twist to turn it. To change it later, open the sticker sheet and tap Move, Resize or Remove, then tap the item.

How do I change a text box?

Select it while arranging and tap the edit button. You can change the words, font, text color and box color, including a see-through box.

How do I use a photo as a background to trace?

Add a reference photo, or take one with the camera, then turn on Trace Over It. The photo sits faintly behind your page while you draw. Turn it off and your drawing stays.

How do I undo?

Tap with two fingers to undo, and with three fingers to redo.

How do I watch a page replay?

Choose More > Replay Sketch. Tap Share as Video to send it to someone.

How do I share my work?

In the More menu you can share a page as an image, both pages as an image, or the whole notebook as a PDF. To share a replay, use Share as Video.

Do my notebooks back up to iCloud?

Yes, when you’re signed in to iCloud. Your notebooks, photos, videos and voice notes back up to your own private storage, and they come back when you reinstall or move to a new device, as long as iCloud is on. A single file over 200 MB stays on the device that made it.

What happens if I delete a notebook or the app?

Deleting a page or notebook in the app also removes it from iCloud. Deleting the app only removes its copy from that device, and your iCloud copy stays.

How do I change my notebook cover or app icon?

In Notebooks, tap the palette on a cover to change it. To change the home screen icon, choose More > App Icon, or use the menu at the top of Notebooks.

Are my notebooks private?

Yes. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we can’t see anything in your iCloud. Read the privacy policy for the details.
