Apps for Every Age

We believe apps should be joyful tools, not time sinks. Every Tiny Bird app is designed with a clear purpose — whether that's sparking a child's imagination, helping a teen learn a skill, or giving a grandparent a delightful hobby.

Our AI-assisted development pipeline means we can go from idea to testable prototype in days, not months. That speed lets us try more ideas and ship only the ones we love.

How We Build Apps

Transparent process, start to finish.

  1. 1

    Community idea

    App ideas come from our community, team, or user feedback. Real needs, real people.

  2. 2

    AI prototyping

    We use AI coding tools to build working prototypes in days. Fast feedback, less waste.

  3. 3

    Human testing

    Real people — including kids and seniors — test every app. We iterate on real feedback.

    Safety review

    Dedicated safety and age-appropriateness review before any public release.
