About Tiny Bird

We believe AI creativity should be honest, open, and human-guided. Here's exactly how we work.

Our Mission

Tiny Bird, Big Dreams was born from a simple belief: imagination shouldn't have age limits. We build apps, storybooks, and websites that bring joy to kids, families, seniors, and everyone in between — using AI as our creative engine and human oversight as our compass.

We're not hiding behind algorithms. Every product on this site was shaped by AI tools, and we tell you exactly which ones, how they were used, and how a human reviewed the output before it reached you.

🌟

Entertain All Ages

From first words to silver years, we build for every moment of life.

The AI Pledge

Five commitments we make to every user.

01

We always disclose AI use

Every product, page, and piece of content carries a clear label indicating if and how AI was used to create it. No exceptions.

02

Humans review everything

No AI output goes live without human review. We check for accuracy, safety, age-appropriateness, and quality before publishing.

03

We name our tools

We publicly list the AI models and platforms we use — currently Claude (Anthropic), Stable Diffusion, and custom workflows.

04

Your data stays yours

We don't train AI models on your personal data, usage patterns, or submitted content without explicit opt-in consent.

05

We improve in public

When we make mistakes — and AI sometimes does — we own them publicly, explain what went wrong, and show how we fixed it.

The Story

Tiny Bird, Big Dreams started as a simple question: "What if we used AI the way it's meant to be used — as a creative partner, not a secret?"

We're a small, passionate team of storytellers, developers, and dreamers who believe that technology works best when it's in service of human connection. Our first storybook took three days with AI assistance. It would have taken three months without it. We shared exactly how we did it. People loved the honesty as much as the story.

That's Tiny Bird. Small team, massive ambition, transparent process. We're building something beautiful — and we want you to see every feather of it.
