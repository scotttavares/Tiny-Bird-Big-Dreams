Support

Stuck in the trenches? Here are answers to the usual questions. Still stuck? Send us a message with your iPhone model and what happened.

How to play

The basics

Tap to hide five stick soldiers and a fort on your half of the page. Rock, Paper, Scissors decides who shoots first. Then take turns: tap a dot where you think the enemy is and fire. A hit scribbles a soldier out; the ink bleeds a little and can spot enemies nearby. First army wiped out loses. New here? Tap Boot Camp on the menu.

Machine gun

A couple of times a game, tap Machine gun, touch one of your soldiers and drag the way he should shoot. The burst runs clear to the edge of the paper.

Boulders, mountains and water

Ink bleed can't pass through boulders or mountains, so they keep soldiers hidden. Soldiers can wade into rivers but not lakes, mountains or boulders.

Two players on iPhone Duo

Stand your iPhone Duo up like a tent between you. Each player sees only their own half of the page, so there's no peeking and no passing the phone. On any other iPhone, pass the phone when the game asks.

Going AWOL

Need to quit a battle? Tap Go AWOL under the page. The battle doesn't count toward your record.

Medals, badges and ranks

Why didn't I get a medal?

Medals are for standout moments only, and no battle gives more than three. Some battles give none: try harder, soldier.

How do I get promoted?

Every battle earns service points, more for wins, hits and wrecked forts. Points add up to promotions from Private all the way to General of the Army. Skill badges add a bonus.

Will my record move to a new phone?

Yes, if you're signed in to iCloud on both. Your record is kept in your own iCloud storage.

Full Arsenal

What does it unlock?

The General (the toughest computer), the Mountains, River and Lake battlefields, Reinforcements and Fort must fall, 5 more soldier voices, every pen plus the colour wheel, the Full Arsenal medal and the One-Man Army badge. It's one purchase, yours to keep.

I bought it but it's locked

Open the Supply depot on the menu and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple ID that bought it.

Can my family use it?

Yes. Full Arsenal works with Family Sharing, so everyone in your family group gets it.

Refunds

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sound

I can't hear anything

Check the ring/silent switch and your volume. Soldier voices can be turned off under More orders on the menu.
