## Description
Lull is a pocket of calm you can open in a single breath.

A living orb breathes with you — growing as you inhale, softening as you exhale — with calming sound and gentle guidance. No account. No ads. No streaks or guilt. Just a minute of calm, whenever you need it.

BREATHE
Follow the orb through proven patterns, each with a clear purpose:
• Calm (4-7-8) — ease anxiety
• Steady (box breathing) — find focus
• Ease & Coherence — everyday balance
• Reset — a 90-second physiological sigh to calm down fast
Or make your own pattern.

MEDITATE
Short guided meditations for real life — Body Scan, Gratitude, and Presence — from 3 to 10 minutes, with soft cues over calming sound.

SLEEP
Longer, slower sessions to help you drift off, fading gently to black.

SOUND
Singing bowls, handpan, binaural tones, and nature — rain, ocean, forest, fire. Bring any of them into a session.

SEE YOUR CALM ADD UP
An optional, private check-in before and after a session shows how much calmer you feel — a quiet trend that proves it’s working. No streaks, no goals, no pressure.

MAKE IT YOURS
A collection of beautiful living orbs, each with its own sky. Unlock packs once — yours forever, no subscription.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your device. No account, no tracking, works fully offline. Export or erase your history anytime.

Lull is made by Tiny Bird, Big Dreams — a tiny studio building calm, honest apps. Breathe easy.
