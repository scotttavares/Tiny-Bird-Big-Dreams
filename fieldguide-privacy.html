Field Guide Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

Field Guide: Sketch Journal is made by Tiny Bird, Big Dreams. Your notebooks are yours. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never receive your sketches, photos, videos, notes or recordings.

Where Your Work Is Kept

Everything you make is stored on your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud, your notebooks, photos, videos and voice notes also sync to your private iCloud storage under your own Apple account, so they reach your other devices. We can't see them. Deleting a page or notebook in the app deletes it from all your devices. Deleting the app only removes its copy from that device.

Camera, Microphone and Location

  • Camera: used only when you take a photo or video, or open the live camera.
  • Microphone: used only when you record a voice note or take a video.
  • Location: used only when you tap “Use current place”. Your phone asks Apple's location service for the place name, and only the name is saved on the page.
  • Photos: the system picker gives the app only the items you choose.

You can turn any of these off at any time in the Settings app.

Sharing

Nothing leaves the app unless you share it yourself. When you do, the app you share to handles it under its own privacy policy.

Children

Field Guide collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
