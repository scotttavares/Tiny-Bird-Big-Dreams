How it works

Gravity for the
people you love

🪐

Add your people

Add the ones who matter by hand, or pull them straight from your Contacts. They start close, right beside you.

⏳

Watch them drift

Each ring is a stretch of time. The longer it’s been since you connected, the further out a person drifts.

2 weeks · a month · 3 · 6 · a year
💬

Reach out in a tap

Send a text through your favorite messenger, or start a call — and they slide right back to the center.

✨

Log the little moments

Ran into them? Grabbed coffee? Log it, and they move closer — right there in their timeline.

Private by design

Yours, and only yours

Orbit has no account and no cloud. Your people, your notes, your history — everything lives on your device and never leaves it. No ads. No tracking. No sign-up.

🔒 On-device only 🙅 No account 📵 No tracking 🏠 Home Screen widget
