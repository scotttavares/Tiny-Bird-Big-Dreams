How it works

Gravity for the people you love

No feeds, no pressure, no streaks to keep. Just a calm way to notice who you're drifting from — and an easy way back.

🫶

Add your people

Pull in friends and family from your Contacts, or add them by hand. They start close, right beside you.

🌗

Notice the drift

The longer it's been since you connected, the further out someone gently drifts — so you can see it before it's too long.

2 weeks · a month · 3 · 6 · a year
💬

Reach out in a tap

Text through your favorite app or start a call, right from Orbit — and they slide warmly back to the center.

✨

Keep the moments

Grabbed lunch? Called on a birthday? Log it, and watch the little history of your friendship grow.

Private by design

Your people stay your people

Orbit has no account and no cloud. Your relationships, your notes, your history — everything lives on your phone and never leaves it. No ads. No tracking. No sign-up. Just you and the people you love.
