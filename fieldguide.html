a sketch journal made for the fold

Field Guide turns your iPhone Duo into a sketchbook that opens like a real one. Draw, stick, scribble and fill two pages with the good stuff.

Coming soon to the App Store Free, with no accounts and no ads.
What’s inside

And that’s just the cover.

Draw your way

Pen, pencil, marker and chalk, in any ink color, on paper that suits the page.

Stick anything

Stickers and washi tape go anywhere. Drag them, spin them and pile them on.

Catch it on video

Short clips play right on your page, next to your photos.

Frame every photo

Film strips, instant photos and kraft mats, with color looks like Sepia and Vivid.

Write it your way

Text boxes in eight fonts, any color, on a colored or see-through box.

Remember the moment

Voice notes, weather and field notes on every page.

Watch it come alive

Replay a page as your strokes draw back and everything slams, swoops and pops into place.

Share the show

Send your replay as a video, or share a finished page.

Pages that turn

Pages turn and curl under your finger, just like paper.

iPhone Duo

Made for the fold.

  • Draw on the right and keep your reference on the left
  • Drag photos and stickers across the fold to copy them
  • Half-fold it like a tent to show off your sketch
  • Play the drawing game together, one on each side
  • Close the phone and the book closes too
Private by design

Your notebooks are yours.

No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never receive your sketches, photos, videos, notes or recordings. Read the privacy policy
