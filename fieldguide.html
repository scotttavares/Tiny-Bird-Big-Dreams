Stickers and tape

Stick anything anywhere.

drag it, spin it, pile it on

Dozens of die-cut stickers and strips of washi tape, ready to pile onto any page. Build a treasure map. Give a garden witch a frog for a face. It’s your book.

Photos and video

Catch it on video.

clips play right on your page

Short videos come alive when you turn to the page. Mount your photos and clips in film strips, instant photos or kraft mats, then try a look like Sepia, Noir or Vivid.

Drawing

Scribble over the good stuff.

four drawing tools, any ink color

Pen, pencil, marker and chalk, in eight field inks or any custom color, at the width you like. Pick your paper color and pattern: blank, lined, dotted, grid or blueprint. Draw on the page or right across your photos. Two fingers to undo, three to redo.

Camera background

Snap it. Trace it.

your camera, behind your paper

Take a photo and it sits faintly behind your page, like tracing paper over the real thing. Sketch that butterfly before it flies, then turn the photo off and keep your drawing.

Text boxes

Say it your way.

eight fonts, every color

Add a text box in any font and color, on a bright box or a see-through one. Then move, stack and spin it like a sticker.

iCloud

Safe in your iCloud.

new phone? your notebooks follow you

When you’re signed in to iCloud, your notebooks, photos, videos and voice notes back up to your own private storage, so they come back when you reinstall or get a new phone. Only you can see them, and there’s no account to make.
