Orbit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Orbit is designed to keep your relationships private. We built it so your data stays with you. This policy explains what that means for the Orbit iOS app.

What we collect

Nothing. Orbit has no account and no server. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Your data stays on your device

The people you add, their details, your notes, and your interaction history are stored only on your device. If you delete the app, that data is removed with it. We never receive a copy.

Contacts

If you choose to import from your address book, Orbit reads your contacts on your device so you can select who to add to your orbit. This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Importing is optional — you can add people manually and use the app fully without granting Contacts access.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Orbit schedules gentle local reminders to reach out. These are generated on your device; there is no push server involved.

Texting & calling

When you tap Send a Text or Quick Call, Orbit simply opens your chosen app (Messages, WhatsApp, Telegram, the Phone app, FaceTime, and so on) with the number filled in. Orbit does not send messages or place calls itself, and does not see your conversations.

Sample data

The optional “sample orbit” loads a few placeholder avatar images from randomuser.me for illustration only. No personal information is sent in these requests, and these are not real people.

Children’s privacy

Orbit does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
