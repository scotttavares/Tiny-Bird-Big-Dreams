Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Paper Wars: Fold & Fire is made by Tiny Bird, Big Dreams. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never receive anything about you or your games.

What the game keeps

Your service record (wins, losses, medals, rank and skill badges), your pen colours and your settings are stored on your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud, the app also keeps a copy in your own iCloud storage under your Apple account, so your record follows you to a new phone. We can't see it.

Permissions

The app doesn't ask for your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location. On iPhone Duo it reads the hinge angle so it knows when the phone is standing like a tent; that never leaves the phone.

Full Arsenal purchase

The Full Arsenal upgrade is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we only learn from Apple whether this Apple ID owns it, so it can unlock. With Family Sharing, your family's purchase counts too.

The web version

The free web version at tinybirdbigdreams.com stores your settings and record only in your own browser.

Children

Paper Wars collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
